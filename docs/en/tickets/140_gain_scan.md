## Reading
- **240 fC is 1/1.87 of 120 fC** (nominally 1/2). Hit ch 127 → 119 and length 88 → 84 mm, so a few weak strips are lost, but **saturation 4.7 % → 0.7 %** and end clip 21 → 9 %.
- **1 pC cannot be used for α** (with the current partial readout + the macros' fixed threshold 35 ADC / STRIP_Q_MIN 2000): only 23 ch exceed the hit register (not read on the hardware side), and the track ok is 0.9 %. Even at 1014 ns the ok is 63 % and the length 48 mm. To use 1 pC, `readout: full` + a revision of the threshold are needed (a matter for C).
- **The wiggle is proportional to the output amplitude** (= rides on the electronics gain): at 1014 ns the ratio 120 fC → 1 pC is 59.9 / 7.5 = 8.0 (≈ 8.5 after subtracting the floor, nominal 8.3), at 502 ns 27.9 / 15.5 = 1.8 (nominal 2). It is not external noise of fixed amplitude. **However, whether it is of electronics or detector origin is not decided by this** (anything proportional to the input charge rides on the gain in either case) → to be decided with the pulser kernels of 138.
- **Setting of E (rule: among saturated events < 2 %, the longest peaking and its gain)**: among those measured, **502 ns × 240 fC (the conditions of run 88)**. 1014 × 240 fC is not measured (judging from 33.7 % at 1014 × 120 fC, 2 % is expected to be tight) → confirm with one additional run (below).
