### Reading
1. **The current partial readout has not reduced a single ch.** The template's `channel.Reading = always` (an unmanaged knob; per the xcfg note, effective when `isAllChannelRead=false` and `enableWriteHittedregister=true`) means all 272 ch are read regardless of the hit register result. Moreover, partial becomes frameType 1 (4 B per sample), so the **data volume is twice that of full (frameType 2 compact, 2 B) (557 vs 279 kB/event)**. The metrics are the same at all points (hit ch 117–121, ok 0.993–0.997, length 82–84 mm) = the thresholds global / lsb change nothing.
2. **ZS (CoBo, uniform 600) passes end to end** (decoder / root-sink / macros): 14.8 kB/event = 1/19 of full, track ok 0.997 and length 86 mm, not inferior. However the ped varies from 364 to 548 ADC, so a uniform 600 is ped +50 to +240 depending on the ch, losing small signals on low-ped ch and leaving noise on high-ped ch. **Per-ch ZS threshold = ped + k·σ (080 D-2, can be made from D's pedestal table)** is next. The analysis side (run_metrics / the 136 lineage) needs ZS support (ped from an external table, assuming no FPN).
3. **Immediate remedy for operation: put base to `readout: full`** (half the data, FPN is read, same metrics). To try true partial readout with the hit register, make a template with `Reading = only_if_hit` (unmanaged knob → `configure-elinp-hit.xcfg`, in the same way as for the pulser) and redo the threshold scan (check whether the number of ch decreases while frameType stays 1).

## Addendum (2026-10-15 20:55, rescan with `Reading = only_if_hit`, runs 135–140, SCANS S15)
With the user's approval, `configure-elinp-hit.xcfg` (elinp with `channel Reading` set to `only_if_hit`) was added to `GetSoftware_config`, and 6 points of global / lsb were taken with the electronics of operating point B. **The hit register works** (557 → 241–400 kB/event, global 4 / lsb 15 is the minimum). The tracks are intact at all points (ok ≥ 0.997, hit ch 118–122, charge 2.07–2.09 M). However, partial is frameType 1 (4 B/sample), so the reduction is at most 14 % relative to full (frameType 2, 279 kB) → **operation stays with full (current B)**. The real compression is ZS (archive/149: 1/8.8, waiting for macros support). The number of ch read itself decreases with the threshold from 557/2 to 241/2 ≈ 43 %, so it would make sense if the FW could do partial in frameType 2 (a zCoBo FW specification, cannot be changed).
