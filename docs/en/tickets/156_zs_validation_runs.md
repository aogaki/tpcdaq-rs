### Verdict
1. **ZS (per-channel ped + 5σ) does not degrade quality**: B + ZS has the same metrics as the reference (hit channels 121 / 121.5, ok 0.998, length 83.9 / 84.4, same dEdx IQR, σ_z 0.20, σ_2D 0.320 / 0.314), with charge −3 % (tails below threshold). **The data is 1/9**.
2. **The FPN may be suppressed (a matter for the user's ruling -> recommendation: "suppress")**: keeping the FPN improves none of the metrics (171 ≈ 170, 173 ≈ 172) and costs +32 kB/event. The value of common-mode correction cannot be seen at the S/N of B.
3. **Hit partial g4 + ZS is the smallest (23 kB, 1/12)**, at the small cost of hit channels −4 and σ_2D +12 % (weak strips are dropped by the hit register). Since only this shortens the dead time (150), the **default candidate for beam runs = hit g4 + ZS (FPN suppressed)**. Source runs: B full (current) or B + ZS.
