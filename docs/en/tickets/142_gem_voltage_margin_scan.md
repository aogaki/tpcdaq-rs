## 1. Principles
- **Do not raise. Only the points that were lowered** (measurement of the discharge margin). The induction TR1 is lowered only, the transfer TR2/3 are not touched.
- Compensate on the electronics side: current 502 ns × 240 fC → 502 × 120 fC (amplitude ×2) → 1014 × 120 fC (×2.3).
- For each point: set on the instrument side → wait ≥ 1 min after the ramp (confirm no trip) → run 1,500 events (5.5 min) → metrics (saturation, hit ch, ok, length, dEdx) → next. The HV of each run is recorded automatically in run_start.slow.

**With a precision of ±5 to 10 %, ln G is explained by the total ΔV** (GEM1 alone at −20 and −10 on each of the 3 foils follow the same law). Statistical error of the slope (median charge over 1,500 events ≈ 1.8 % → ±0.026 in ln): b 0.0151 ± 0.0026, c 0.0150 ± 0.0013, d 0.0143 ± 0.0009 → the difference b–d, 0.0008 ± 0.0028, is not significant. Systematics: a (250 V) has saturation 4.7 % so its charge is underestimated (making the ratio larger), and as the gain drops the number of strips falling below the 35 ADC threshold increases (127 → 121 ch, making the slope larger). **Differences in the coefficient per foil or non-linearity cannot be judged at this precision** (user's remark 2026-10-15). To pin it down: points with GEM2 / GEM3 alone (146), and a re-evaluation with an estimator insensitive to saturation and threshold (the median of the ch charge of medium strips). One step of the electronics (120 → 240 fC = ×1/2, the peak ratio 1014 → 502 ns ≈ ×1/2.3) corresponds to 46–56 V of GEM total.

### Conclusion (Fable)
1. **Discharge margin** (within the precision above): from the current 250 × 3, either −20 V on one foil or −10 V on all three, compensated on the electronics side (120 fC / 1014 ns), leaves the track quality (ok, hit ch, length, relative width of dEdx ≈ 0.49) unchanged. = **the operating point can be lowered with a margin of at least 10 V/foil (30 V in total)**. No trips or current anomalies over the 7 runs.
2. **Conversion table**: each −10 V of GEM total gives total charge ×0.86. The electronics have two steps, 240 fC → 120 fC (×2) and 502 → 1014 ns (peak ×2.3), which together can absorb about 100 V of GEM total.
3. **Recommended operating point**: **240 × 3 (the same value as the HIGS main detector) + 502 ns × 120 fC** (saturation 1.0 %, hit ch 121, ok 1.000). The performance equals 250 × 3 + 502 × 240 fC (saturation 0.7 %, 119 ch), with GEM voltages 10 V/foil lower. Adoption and the change of the base params are the user's decision (when changing base, set the gain of `base_1750.json` to 120fC).
4. Not done: point e that changes the distribution (raising GEM3) was excluded under the "do not raise" policy. TR1 (induction) was not touched.
