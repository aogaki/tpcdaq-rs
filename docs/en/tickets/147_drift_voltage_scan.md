### Reading
1. **v_drift ∝ E**: time width × V is constant (±6 %, within the 1-cell resolution of the median). The relative E dependence of v is linear and not inconsistent with the nominal formula v = 0.7233 × E/p (the absolute value remains the homework of 131).
2. **Diffusion**: pulse width 61 → 57 → 54 cells (−11 % for E +40 %). 1/√E would give −15 %, which is consistent considering that the shaping width (≈ 20 cells) is added. The behaviour of the thermal region of CO2.
3. **Focusing onto GEM3**: the total charge and hit ch are the same at the 3 points (2.07–2.09 M, 120–123 ch) → no loss at 50–70 V/cm.
4. **σ_z is constant in mm (0.207 / 0.208 / 0.208)**, and in cells proportional to 1/v. = the z error of a hit is determined by the "precision in distance", not the "precision in time". Candidate: a geometric spread from the strip width × track inclination (dip ≈ 7°, ≈ 0.18 mm for a pitch ≈ 1.5 mm). This is consistent with the result of 146 that it hardly depends on any of peaking, HV, or E. The worsening at 1014 ns (0.31 mm) is the time precision of the electronics added in quadrature (√(0.31² − 0.21²) ≈ 0.23 mm).
5. Operation: no problem staying at 1,200 V. 1,400 V is slightly advantageous in window occupancy and diffusion, but there is no difference in σ_z or charge, so there is little reason to change. **Return the drift to 1,200 V (600 / 600)**.
