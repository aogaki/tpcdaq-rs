## 0. Questions
1. Is the "wiggle" of the signal ch seen in 137 (3–7 µs after the peak, including a −20 ADC dip, growing with peaking in the same ratio as the charge) **due to the electronics (shaper) or to the detector (ion induction)**?
   If the same shape appears in the pulser response it is the electronics → can be removed by deconvolution. If not, it is the detector.
2. The **impulse response** (mean waveform) of the 4 peaking values (70 / 232 / 502 / 1014 ns) and linearity (2 amplitudes). = the kernel of 139.

### Answers to the questions
1. **The α "wiggle" (137: signal ch only, period ≈ 1.6 µs, −20 ADC, proportional to charge) is not a response of the AGET shaping system.** The kernel injected through the test capacitance has at most 0.03 % periodic component. → The origin is on the detector side (coupling of strips / input network, ion induction) or in the input network before the injection point. **It cannot be removed by deconvolution with the electronics kernel**.
   - Note: the sustained 1.8 µs oscillation seen in runs 91–98 at 14:00 (template `nothing`, no injection, only the generator operating at a 50 ms period) is a **coupling in which the generator's operation leaks into the ch** (it remains in the signal − FPN difference). Always take pulser runs with a template that has injection.
2. **The kernels were obtained** (`mean_norm` 341 points × 4 peaking, 2 amplitudes −300 / −100). Linearity: −300/−100 = 2.84–2.87 (nominal 3.0, the same trend as 2.91 in 091 = ≈ 5 % compression on the high-amplitude side). Rise and FWHM are proportional to peaking; at 70 ns it is set by the sampling (40 ns). The peak position is delayed by the peaking difference (+3 / +10 / +24 cells). → Input of 139.
