## 0. Questions (archive/109 had 182 Hz = the 1 GbE limit with partial (frameType 1, 557 kB/event))
1. Is the limit of B (full, 279 kB/event) the FW (2.93 ms -> 340 Hz) or the network (360 Hz)?
2. Does the hit-register partial (`only_if_hit`, global 4 = 43 % of channels read) **shorten the readout time from 2.93 ms** (if so, dead time can be bought)?
3. What is the limit of ZS (32 kB/event) (the network is out of the question -> FW and root_sink CPU)?

Reading:
- **The limit of B full is the FW readout time**: the inter-event interval = period + **2.938 ms** at every point, and even at a 1 ms period the maximum interval is 3.9 ms = the median (no disturbance from network back-pressure). 279 kB × 254 Hz = 71 MB/s, comfortably within 1 GbE. **With a periodic trigger the interval is "period + readout"**, so the achieved rate is 254 Hz, and the 2.94 ms readout is the dead time.
- **The hit-register partial shortens the readout time**: with no signal (channels read ≈ 16 FPN lines) it is **0.283 ms**. Since 272 ch gives 2.938 ms, **readout time ≈ 0.11 ms + 10.4 µs × number of channels read**. For a real alpha event (hit channels ≈ 120 + 16 FPN) it is ≈ 1.5 ms = half the dead time.
- ZS + periodic trigger: with no signal the CoBo emits no frame at all (stall) — a ZS rate test needs a signal -> second round with the pulser (−300 mV on all 256 ch).

## Conclusions (Fable)
1. **The limit is the FW readout time, not the network**: **2.94 ms** for 272 ch (= 340 Hz, 1 AsAd). Same for full / ZS. 1 GbE has room up to ≈ 340 Hz for full (279 kB), and was touched at its edge by the 1 ms pulser (310 Hz, 86 MB/s).
2. **Triggers during readout are lost** (not queued): pulser 2 ms -> every other one. = dead time = rate × readout time. At beam 100 Hz this is **29 %** for full.
3. **Readout time is proportional to the number of channels read**: ≈ 0.11 ms + 10.4 µs × ch (16 ch 0.28 ms, 272 ch 2.94 ms). **The hit-register partial (`only_if_hit`) is the only means that shortens the dead time.** For a real alpha event (≈ 136 ch) ≈ 1.5 ms -> 15 % at 100 Hz. However, partial is frameType 1 (4 B/sample), and if all channels are hit it is 557 kB = stuck at 170 Hz on 1 GbE.
4. **ZS reduces only bytes** (readout time unchanged). Even with all channels hit it is 65 kB (1/4.3 of full; 1/8.8 for alpha events). **Partial + ZS are compatible**, so both the dead time (partial) and the bandwidth and disk (ZS) are gained.
5. **Optical fibre / 10 GbE do not help the zCoBo link** (the FW's MAC is 1 GbE, with a 1 GbE adapter in the SFP+). The PC-side 10 GbE already exists for receiving a second zCoBo.
6. **Estimate for Warsaw (2 boards × 2 AsAd)**: 544 ch per board -> readout ≈ 5.8 ms -> maximum 170 Hz, dead time 58 % at 100 Hz (full). With partial (occupancy 25 %, ≈ 150 ch) ≈ 1.7 ms -> 17 %. Bandwidth: with one 1 GbE per board, full 558 kB × 170 Hz = 95 MB/s (at the edge); with partial + ZS there is ample room. **Design for beam 100 Hz = hit-register partial + ZS + two 1 GbE links**. The FW readout time (is it really 2× for 2 AsAd?) must be measured on site at Warsaw.
7. The "400 Hz" for mini is physically impossible with the FW's 340 Hz (1 AsAd). Reducing channels with partial gets up to 779 Hz (FPN only), so it **depends on occupancy**.
