### Time evolution (175, 1-hour bins, statistical accuracy ±0.5 %)
- Median charge: 0–1 h 2.05 M -> slowly falls from 2.04 to 2.00 M over 1–6 h -> **flat at 2.00 ± 0.01 M from 6 to 68 h**. No day-night periodicity, no monotonic drift. The 16 % point also goes 1.39 -> 1.34 M (−3.5 %), so the whole distribution shrinks uniformly. In the user's chargeVsTime.C too, the spectra of the first and last quarters have the same shape.
- Hit channels 121 -> 120 -> 119 (±1). Band position first 75 / last 98 cells, pulse width 56 cells, charge-centroid time 90 cells: **unchanged = drift velocity and diffusion do not change**.
- Rate 4.42 -> 4.33 Hz (−2 %), back to 4.40 after 48 h. Saturation fraction 0.8–1.2 %, no change.
- HV (InfluxDB, 1 h average, 69 points): vmon ±0.05 V on all channels, imon ±0.5 % (GEM1 ch1 8.57–8.64 µA). No periodicity and no slope.

### Verdict
1. With the flow going from 100 to 50 ml/min, **total charge −3.5 %, dEdx −4 %** (a decrease in gain or collection efficiency), settling into a new equilibrium within a few hours and then stable for 60 h. Track quality (ok, length, dip) is unchanged.
2. HV is irrelevant. Since drift velocity and diffusion are unchanged, it is not a large change of gas composition but an impurity equilibrium or a slight change of density. There is no record of temperature and pressure, so it cannot be separated (the environment sensor of 131 B-2).
   **Correction of units (2026-10-19, user)**: the flow is in ml/min (not ml/s), and the chamber volume is 54 L. 50 ml_n/min at 91 mbar is an actual flow of ≈ 0.56 L/min -> **volume-exchange time constant ≈ 1.6 h (≈ 5 h for 95 %)**, so the observed "equilibrium in 6 hours" is explained by simple volume exchange (there is no need to bring in wall desorption). For 100 ml/min it would be 0.8 h, for 150 it would be 0.54 h.
3. Operationally 50 ml/min is fine. After changing the flow, **do not cross a calibration for 6 hours**.
