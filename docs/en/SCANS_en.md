## Rules (decided from the scans)
- **triggerDelay**: put the start of the band ≈ 2.8 µs before the trigger → `delay[10 ns] = (window[ns] − 2,800 − 160) / 10` (25 MHz: 1750, 12.5 MHz: 3800). L (trigger lag) ≈ 0.16 µs + peaking.
- **peaking × gain (E rule)**: the longest peaking, and its gain, for which events containing saturated ch are < 2 %. At the present HV, 502 ns × 240 fC.
- **Total charge** is fully captured by the "sum of samples" at any peaking (S8). What changes with peaking is the peak height (saturation, detection of weak strips) and the time width.
